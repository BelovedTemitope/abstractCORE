Levels of abstraction

  • The idea What the system is for
  • Core parts The major parts and how they relate
  • How it works Behaviour and the flow of data
  • Functions and code Optional. There when you want it, never required

What stays in your hands

  • Decisions only you can make are flagged, and nothing is built around the gap.
  • Every AI choice is on the record with its reasoning and alternatives, in one ledger you can review at any time.
  • Change anything at the level it was made. Override an AI decision and the parts beneath it reshape.
